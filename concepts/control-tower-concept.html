02

Review of the reference

Verdict: the reference is a strong operational snapshot — right panels, clean hierarchy, honest use of an action center. Its one structural gap is that all fourteen widgets describe the past or the present; nothing on the screen answers “what breaks next?”. The redesign keeps its bones and adds the missing tense.

✓Keep — it earns its place

  • KPI strip with targets (Fulfilment vs 95%)
  • Action Center — counts by category
  • Today's schedule with live statuses
  • Client Health as a table, not a chart
  • Pipeline funnel of open orders

✕Fix — quiet problems

  • Donut duplicates the funnel — same 679 orders, same stages, twice on one screen. Drop it.
  • Recent Orders table repeats the Orders page. A link, not a panel.
  • Numbers disagree — 24 deliveries today vs 5 on the schedule; 679 pipeline vs 186 revenue orders. Consistency is trust.
  • Health scores are static — 86 means nothing without direction. Add the 30-day arrow.

＋Add — the missing tense

  • A forecast line on every KPI — “96.8%, trending to 94.6% by month-end”
  • The Radar band: budget-breach dates, stock run-outs in days, capacity overloads
  • Horizon switch — Today / 7d / 30d re-aims the whole board
  • Bottleneck detection on the pipeline, not just counts
  • Action queue ranked by cost of waiting, not category
03

Arithmetic, not AI

Every forecast on the radar is a run-rate projection over data Smart Pantry already stores. No models to train, no black boxes to explain to a client — and each one can graduate to something smarter later without changing the UI.

₹Budget breach dates

Daily burn = month-to-date spend ÷ days elapsed. Project to month-end; if projected > budget, solve for the crossing date. Reads orders + clients.monthly_budget — the same query the client dashboard already runs, extended one line.

☕Stock run-outs

Days of cover = current stock ÷ daily draw (14-day average from client_consumption and order lines). Sort ascending; anything under the horizon makes the radar. The Order all low-stock button you already shipped is the one-click fix.

🚚Capacity overloads

The Delivery Calendar already knows booked DCs, recurring ghosts and fleet capacity per day. The radar simply sums booked + projected per day ahead and flags days over the line — the chart in the mock is that exact query.

⛓Bottleneck detection

Average time between statuses in order_history, per stage, this week vs trailing month. The slowest stage gets named on the funnel. Cheap to compute, disproportionately useful to ops.

♥Health trajectory

Recompute the health score over the trailing 30 days and keep last month's value — the arrow is just the sign of the difference, and the hover explains which input moved (budget pace, fulfilment, due orders).
